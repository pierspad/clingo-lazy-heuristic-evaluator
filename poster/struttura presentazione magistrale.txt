

Good morning everyone, my name is Pierpaolo Spadafora and this is my thesis work on the development of a clingo propagator to lazy evaluate heuristics.

In ASP, domain heuristics are a way for the programmer to guide the solver with a specific search criterion.

They are written in the ASP program under the form of a rule, THEN they are either:
grounded, meaning their FOL representation is transformed into a propositional program, before it gets passed to the solver in G&S solvers such as clingo
or 
in lazy ground solvers, like alpha, they are used as a "live guidance" where the two aforementioned phases are interleaved.

G&S solvers are generally way faster but when a heuristic directive needs information related to the status of the search the rule must be written in a way that it's ground output will cover every possible situation of the saerch and this can easily explode combinatorically

This makes, a measure added to speed up the solving process, in something that makes it untractable

To have the best of both worlds we created a custom propagator, a program that uses the ::Heuristic interface provided by clingo to interact in the different phases of the solving process.

We initiate by rewriting the heuristic directive

e.g.
#heuristic b(X) : x(X), not c(X), S = #sum{Y : c(Y)}, W = ((n+1)*S)+X. [W, true]


in a ground rule which scope is to describe how the heuristic is written rather than it's meaning

__heuristic(__target(b), x, __n_c, __bind(s, __sum(c)),
            __weight(__add(__mul(s, B), self)),
            __semantics(clingo), __modifier(true)) :- B = n + 1.

we distinguish positive and negative literals in the heuristic body with a __n_c:
x
__n_c

use some shorthands to mathematical operations:
__add
__sub
__mul
(altro?)

bind values to variables with the __bind literal:
__bind(s, __sum(c))


And some other parameters that will be clear in a minute

__semantics(clingo), 
__modifier(true)
